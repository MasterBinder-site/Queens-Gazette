Royal Grid Support

Need help with Royal Grid: Queens Puzzle? You can report a bug, gameplay issue or other problem through the developer contact below.

Developer: Cato Martinussen

Email: masterbinderapp@gmail.com

Before reporting a problem

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Royal Grid app version, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot can also be helpful for visual or puzzle-related issues.

Gameplay

Place exactly one queen in every row, column and colored region. Queens may not touch diagonally. If Help is enabled, the board can visually mark cells blocked by queens you have already placed.
