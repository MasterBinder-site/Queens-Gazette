How to play

Place exactly one queen in every row, every column and every colored region. Queens may not touch each other diagonally.

Help & Mistakes

The optional Help feature marks cells blocked by queens you have already placed. The Mistakes counter tracks placements that leave the current puzzle with no valid solution.

Advertising & privacy

Queens Gazette uses banner advertising only. Where available, advertising privacy choices can be opened from Settings inside the app.

Need more help?

If you experience a problem, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Queens Gazette version and a short description of what happened when contacting the developer through the App Store listing.
