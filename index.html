A clean Queens logic puzzle

Royal Grid is a free logic puzzle game built around a simple challenge: place exactly one queen in every row, column and region, while making sure queens do not touch diagonally.

Play your way

Choose Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert and generate new puzzles whenever you want. Royal Grid also includes a Daily Puzzle, statistics, streak tracking and optional Help.

No forced interstitial ads. No subscription. Core gameplay is free.
