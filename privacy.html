QUEENS GAZETTE

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Overview

Queens Gazette does not require an account. Game progress, settings and statistics are stored locally on your device.

Advertising

Queens Gazette uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising data and interaction data in order to provide, measure and protect advertising services.

Where required, the app uses Google's consent management platform to request consent or provide privacy choices before advertising data is processed.

Data stored by the app

The app may store gameplay information on your device, including settings, games played, completed puzzles, best times and Daily Puzzle streaks. This information is used to provide app functionality and is not used to create a Queens Gazette account.

Third-party services

Advertising is provided by Google AdMob. Google's own privacy policies govern information processed by Google through its services.

Your choices

If privacy options are required in your region, they can be accessed from the app's Settings screen. You can also remove locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device.

Children

Queens Gazette is a general-audience puzzle game and is not designed to collect personal information directly from users.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app or the services it uses change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact method listed on the Queens Gazette App Store page or the support page.
