A classic logic challenge

Queens Gazette is a logic puzzle game built around one simple challenge: place exactly one queen in every row, column and region while making sure queens do not touch diagonally.

Choose your difficulty, play unlimited generated puzzles, or return for the Daily Puzzle and build your streak.

Simple by design

Queens Gazette focuses on the puzzle. The game includes multiple difficulty levels, optional help, mistake tracking, statistics and daily challenges.
